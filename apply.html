Call for Applications

The Vishvanath Sathe Fellowship

Nath Valley School, Aurangabad

A three-month visiting fellowship placing an early-career researcher in mathematics, physics, or a related quantitative discipline on a school campus—to pursue their own research while renewing students’ engagement with the scientific process.

Duration
July – October 2026
Disciplines
Mathematics, Physics, and related quantitative disciplines
Stipend
Comparable to a senior postdoctoral position in India
Location
Nath Valley School, Aurangabad
Eligibility
  • Hold a PhD from a leading national or international university
  • Have peer-reviewed publications in reputable journals
  • Demonstrate enthusiasm for science communication and teaching
Application Deadline
15 June 2026
